## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are greenhouse cannabis operators, cultivation leads, HVAC and greenhouse design partners, and investor/operator reviewers. They use the dashboard to pressure-test mixed-light production strategy, compare equipment paths, and understand where light, climate, and operating cost become limiting.

### Brand Personality
Technical, trustworthy, and plainspoken. The interface should feel like an engineering cockpit: confident, data-dense, and serious without becoming sterile or academic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode only for now. Editorial engineering layout with strong hierarchy, concise labels, and clear workspaces. Avoid gimmicky cannabis styling, generic SaaS gradients, or playful dashboard tropes. The visual model should feel prominent and useful, not decorative.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the decision surface: users should find the live greenhouse model and the primary work areas immediately.
2. Explain the dashboard like a tool, not a demo: every major section needs a title and a short plain-language statement of what it helps decide.
3. Preserve engineering honesty: keep units visible, keep warnings readable, and avoid decorative visuals that imply more certainty than the model can support.
4. Use hierarchy instead of clutter: tighter grouping for related controls, more space between distinct work areas, and emphasis on the few views people should use first.
5. Keep the tone operator-friendly: sophisticated enough for consultants and investors, legible enough for growers moving quickly.
